🌐 What is IPFS?

IPFS (InterPlanetary File System) is a decentralized storage network. Your encrypted data is stored across multiple nodes, identified by a unique CID (Content Identifier).

Important: Store your encryption keys safely! Without them, your data cannot be recovered.
